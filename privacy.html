Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how this application ("the App") handles data.

1. Data Collected

The App accesses TikTok account data (such as access tokens and basic profile information) only for accounts explicitly authorized by their owner through TikTok's official OAuth login flow.

2. How Data Is Used

Access tokens are used solely to publish video content to the authorized TikTok account(s) on behalf of the account owner. No data is shared with, or sold to, any third party.

3. Data Storage

Access tokens and related credentials are stored locally on the developer's own device and are not transmitted to any external server other than TikTok's official API endpoints.

4. Video Content

Video files uploaded through the App are read from local folders owned by the developer and are sent directly to TikTok's API for publishing. No video content is stored on any third-party server.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Stored tokens can be deleted at any time by removing them from the local configuration file. Revoking access via TikTok's account settings will also invalidate the App's access.

6. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer through the app registration details on file with TikTok for Developers.
